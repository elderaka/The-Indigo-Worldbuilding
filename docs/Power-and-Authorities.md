# Power, Authorities & Alter Matter

**Status:** Mixture of recent creator-confirmed concepts and **legacy** terminology. Some rosters changed; consult the status notes.

## Three conceptual domains

The older power-system documents arrange power into **Laws**, **Rules**, and **Orders**. This remains a useful organizing distinction, but the definitive roster and boundaries of each are undergoing revision.

| Domain | Broad remit | Office names from older material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Laws of the Universe** | Constraints that make physical/cosmic reality function | **Lawyer**, **Overseer** |
| **Rules of Life** | Metaphysical restrictions or conditions tied to living beings | **Ruler**, **Overlord** |
| **Orders of Nature** | The structured classification of phenomena that Arts users can manipulate | **Ordinator**, **Overwatch** |

These labels and the meanings of specific offices must not be conflated. In particular, **Creator / Creationist** is a different category from **Lawyer / Ruler / Ordinator**, and one individual may occupy more than one office.

### Laws

The creator says that the **Laws themselves are already satisfactorily defined**; no revision should be forced merely to make the system look more like RPG magic.

In the **older power-system document**, the named Law domains included **Time & Space**, **Gravity**, **Matter (Hal)**, **Energy (Enerji)**, **Reality**, **Infinity**, and **Nullity**.

**Important current correction:** The creator describes a **hidden Lawyer of Energy**, who exists but **is not part of the recognized seven**. Therefore the older list should **not** be pasted as the current seven-seat roster without reconciliation. The identity and precise official structure remain unstated here.

Other established examples: **Eros Rai** is Overseer of Time (also referred to as Time and Space), and **Styx Rai** is a Lawyer and an Absolute Being.

### Rules and Rulers

The older list included **Mind**, **Fate**, **Figure**, **Knowledge**, **Soul**, **Mortal**, and **Power**. The creator has since explicitly corrected **Ada Cherubim's domain to Ruler of Art**, meaning **creative expression and imagination**, **not** magical power as such.

**Do not automatically substitute Art for Power in the final official seven**: whether the complete Rule roster changed and what was displaced remain open.

Pandora is identified as both **Lawyer and Ruler**, as well as Creationist. The possible overlap of his office with Ada's curse has not been exhaustively explained.

### Orders

Older draft: Orders are broad, nature-derived “element” categories, including fire/high temperature, ice/low temperature, liquid matter, solid matter, gaseous matter, carbon/organic matter, plasma/electric, and luminance/light-dark.

The creator now questions whether this taxonomy is too **JRPG-like**, given that Arts fundamentally involve manipulating **three-dimensional matter and phenomena**.

**Not yet canonized:** Replacing those disciplines with a rigorous modern-physics property system. That was a design suggestion, not an adopted final rule.

The creator has also described the **Orders as emerging after the conflict following Ada's creation of Alter Matter**. Their historical function and organization need further retelling.

## Alter Matter / Art Particles

**Alter Matter** (also called **Art Particles**) is the extraordinary medium of Arts. The creator describes it as matter which **violates the ordinary Law of Matter** and can be altered into different Orders/phenomena.

- Ada is described as **its inventor/creator**, during events surrounding an attempt to destroy the universe.
- Its existence helps explain why ordinary physical constraints and magical phenomena can coexist.
- The exact conservation principles and interactions with the **hidden Lawyer of Energy** are **not yet specified**.

Avoid substituting purely real-world conservation laws for this fictional mechanism or assuming Alter Matter is automatically literal antimatter. “Antimatter” also appears as a named technique in legacy writing, and different usages need to be kept separate.

## Cross-project boundary

The **Spellware Engineering** game explores programmable spells with **Codes**, **Crowns**, **Formae**, **Grimoires**, and **Art Boards**. Those are game-system designs. They are **not** automatically universal requirements for how all Arts users in all Indigo installments work.

This repository records **setting-level metaphysics** and narrative canon. Do not import implementation-dependent casting constraints without explicit creator confirmation.
