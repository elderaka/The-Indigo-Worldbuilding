# Open Questions, Retcons & Editorial Backlog

**Purpose:** Preserve uncertainty. This is an **authoring queue**, not a claim that every question needs an answer before the story can continue.

## Known legacy-versus-current differences

**1. Ada's Rule**

- **Legacy:** Ruler of Power.
- **Recent creator confirmation:** **Ruler of Art** — imagination and creative expression, not a mage class or simple synonym for Arts.
- **Action:** Preserve older wording only with a *Legacy* label.

**2. Creationist wings**

- **Legacy shorthand:** winged Creationists, Pandora zero-winged, Ada twelve-winged.
- **Recent creator confirmation:** **halos with plates resembling wings**. Pandora displays two halos, with an outer halo comprising an indeterminate plate count. Ada obtained her twelve sets through accelerated iterations rather than ordinary original creation.
- **Action:** Illustrate halos, not literal bird wings, when reconstructing this design.

**3. Enerji / Energy**

- **Legacy:** Energy listed alongside the named seven Laws and an Overseer.
- **Recent creator confirmation:** A **hidden Lawyer of Energy** exists **outside the recognized seven**.
- **Action:** Do not assert a final official seven-seat list until retold.

**4. Orders**

- **Legacy:** Nature-based elemental taxonomy.
- **Current discussion:** Creator considers the system too JRPG-like; revised property/phenomenon-based framing has been proposed but not adopted.
- **Action:** Keep revisions visibly labeled *Proposal*.

**5. The Indigo's “winning”**

- **Tempting but incorrect shorthand:** The Indigo cannot die or is guaranteed happiness.
- **Recent creator confirmation:** **Fate ensures achievement of an objective**, possibly even if the world is decimated.
- **Action:** Never claim survival or happy ending is inherently guaranteed.

**6. Ada's powers**

- **Tempting extrapolation:** Tier 0 means omnipotence, every alternative is fake, or Fate is impossible to resist.
- **Current status:** Ada's cross-media meta-level reach is intentional, but her reliable limits are **undecided**.
- **Action:** Keep suggestions about narrative constraints outside canon until approved.

## Editorial rules for new entries

Use the following header on new lore pages:

```md
# Entry name

Status: Creator-confirmed | Legacy | Proposal | Unknown
Scope: Cosmology | Character | Authority | History | Installment
Source: Creator conversation / older manuscript / newer recovered material

## Established
...

## Unresolved
...
```

**No invented bridges:** If two versions conflict, cite both and ask the creator later. Avoid inferring character identities, positions, chronology, lineage, and motives from name similarities alone.

**Spelling convention:** Preserve the creator's most recent explicit form; older spellings may appear as searchable aliases, but do not silently merge people with similar names.

## When more lore is shared

Start with a short, faithful capture of what was said. Only then create backlinks and possibly reconcile older material. It is better to have five reliable pages than fifty pages of invented consistency.
