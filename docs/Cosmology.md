# Cosmology: Timelines, Beings, and Dimensions

**Status:** Creator-confirmed terminology; relationships and edge cases are **open**.

## The four classifications

| Term | Working definition given by the creator | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prime Timeline** | The singular, uniquely designated Prime Timeline | No specific identifier established |
| **Prime Being** | Only one instance of that being exists within the universe | Specific instance not yet designated here |
| **Absolute Being** | At least one such being exists in each timeline / universe | **Styx Rai**, a Lawyer |
| **Omni-Being** | A Prime Being that nonetheless exists in **every universe** | **Eros Rai**, Overseer of Time |

The apparently paradoxical Omni-Being label is intentional: it combines *uniqueness* with *cross-universe existence*. Whether that means a single continuous consciousness, distributed manifestations, or something else is **not yet settled**.

The creator refers to a multiverse of timelines/universes while distinguishing the **Prime Timeline** as one-and-only. Do not interpret this as meaning that no other timelines exist.

## Thirteen dimensions

The Indigo setting contains **13 dimensions**, grouped as follows:

| Dimension indices | Domain | Established examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1–3** | Space | The three spatial dimensions |
| **4–6** | Soul | Heaven, Hell, other soul/spiritual dimensions |
| **7–9** | Time | Temporal dimensions |
| **10–12** | Reality | Reality dimensions |
| **13** | Infinite | Infinite Dimension |

This is the creator's metaphysical setting taxonomy. It should not be rewritten as a real-world physics claim. The hierarchy between these groups, travel rules, and what “Infinite” operationally means remain **undetermined**.

## Where this intersects with Fate

Prime Timeline, Prime Being, and Omni-Being status matter because the setting includes Lawyers and Rulers, whose actions can affect universe-scale conditions, alongside Fate-selected Indigos and simulation-surviving The A.

**Unknown:** Whether the Prime Timeline has priority over all branches, whether outcomes are fixed throughout the entire dimensional system, and whether an Omni-Being can perceive or affect every version at once.
