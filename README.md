# The Indigo — Worldbuilding Archive

> **Work in progress · Story spoilers · Provisional recovery**

This repository is the independent worldbuilding reference for **The Indigo Project**, a setting spanning narrative fiction, games, and potentially animation. It is **not** the Spellware Engineering game repository: the two projects may share concepts, but their design documents and canon must not be conflated.

This is an **initial snapshot**, reconstructed from the creator's November 2026 discussions. Only a small fraction of the larger story has been discussed. This repository must therefore **never be treated as a complete world bible**.

## Evidence and canon policy

- **Creator-confirmed**: stated directly in recent worldbuilding discussion, including explicit corrections to older versions.
- **Legacy**: present in older story drafts or power-system documents, **not automatically current canon**.
- **Interpretation / Proposal**: a reading or suggestion made during discussion; should **never silently become canon**.
- **Unknown**: not yet told or not finalized. Don't fill it by extrapolating from genre conventions.

**Source precedence:** newest explicit creator correction > earlier creator discussion > older documents/diary > assistant interpretation. Preserve conflicts and ambiguities rather than artificially reconciling them.

### Original materials

The original creator also has a personal, experimental *Life Diary* (2019–2023), an older *Indigo Power System* document, and early *The Indigo* fiction drafts. These are **historical primary sources** and may contain outdated names, rankings, and plot decisions. Their full text is intentionally **not republished here**.

## Working principles

1. Keep **universe lore** distinct from **game implementations**.
2. Do not make Ada the center of every installment; she is one recurring antagonist and an occasional meta-level anomaly.
3. Record rules precisely before extending cosmology or powerscaling.
4. Keep spoilers and unresolved history visible as such.
5. Update pages as the creator retells the wider story; preserve legacy versions instead of erasing them.
